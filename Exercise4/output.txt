=========================================================
Exercise 4 - Hadoop MapReduce Word Count on Multiple Files
=========================================================

nandi@NandeeshHM:~$ start-dfs.sh

nandi@NandeeshHM:~$ jps
NameNode
DataNode
SecondaryNameNode
Jps

nandi@NandeeshHM:~$ hdfs dfs -mkdir /my_data_directory

nandi@NandeeshHM:~$ echo "This is the content of file1.txt" > file1.txt

nandi@NandeeshHM:~$ echo "This is the content of file2.txt" > file2.txt

nandi@NandeeshHM:~$ echo "This is the content of file3.txt" > file3.txt

nandi@NandeeshHM:~$ hdfs dfs -put file1.txt /my_data_directory/

nandi@NandeeshHM:~$ hdfs dfs -put file2.txt /my_data_directory/

nandi@NandeeshHM:~$ hdfs dfs -put file3.txt /my_data_directory/

nandi@NandeeshHM:~$ hdfs dfs -ls /my_data_directory
Found 3 items
-rw-r--r--   1 nandi supergroup 33 file1.txt
-rw-r--r--   1 nandi supergroup 33 file2.txt
-rw-r--r--   1 nandi supergroup 33 file3.txt

nandi@NandeeshHM:~$ hdfs dfs -cat /my_data_directory/file1.txt
This is the content of file1.txt

nandi@NandeeshHM:~$ hdfs dfs -cat /my_data_directory/file2.txt
This is the content of file2.txt

nandi@NandeeshHM:~$ hdfs dfs -cat /my_data_directory/file3.txt
This is the content of file3.txt

nandi@NandeeshHM:~$ hadoop jar $HADOOP_HOME/share/hadoop/mapreduce/hadoop-mapreduce-examples-*.jar wordcount /my_data_directory /wordcount_output

Job completed successfully.

nandi@NandeeshHM:~$ hdfs dfs -cat /wordcount_output/part-r-00000
This    3
content 3
file1.txt       1
file2.txt       1
file3.txt       1
is      3
of      3
the     3

nandi@NandeeshHM:~$ hdfs dfs -cat /wordcount_output/part-r-00000 | sort -t$'\t' -k2 -nr | head -3
the     3
of      3
is      3

Exercise completed successfully.
